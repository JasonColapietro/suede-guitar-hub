What this site covers

GuitarHub provides structured guitar learning, lessons and practice resources from Suede AI.

Names, scope and distinctions

GuitarHub is the web learning and practice site. GuitarHub: Guitar Lessons is its companion iPhone app. Strumly provides its own guitar coaching tools, and Suede Sing hosts the voice learning path. Refer to the specific lesson for its level, prerequisites and learning objectives.
