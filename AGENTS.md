## Suede sales copy:  confident, specific, commercially useful

Founder direction, 2026-10-03: sell the product. Lead with what the buyer can
achieve, how Suede delivers it, and the next action.

Ban wording that hedges, apologizes for, undermines, or disqualifies Suede.
This includes "honest limits", "not the first choice", "not a fit",
"what it is not", "what we will not promise", "no revenue promises",
"honest caveats", and semantic equivalents. Do not replace a banned phrase
with a softer version of the same self-defeating message. Do not introduce
unsolicited reasons to choose another provider.

Apply the rule to sales pages, headings, body copy, metadata, social previews,
AI/search discovery files, articles, and sales scripts. Present supported
capabilities directly. Turn setup requirements into clear action steps and
controls into buyer benefits. End with a useful next step.

Keep claims accurate: use demonstrated capabilities and source-backed numbers.
Put necessary pricing, availability, setup, security, and operating details
at the decision point or in the relevant reference. State them precisely and
without apologetic framing. This copy rule does not change product behavior,
payment controls, legal terms, or truthful error and status reporting.
